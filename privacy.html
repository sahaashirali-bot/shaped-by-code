Privacy policy

This policy describes the current Shaped by Code website. Last updated: September 14, 2026.

Information you enter

The quote form asks for your name, company name, email address, phone number, and requested service. When you submit the quote form, the details you enter are sent to Formspree for delivery to Shaped by Code and email notification. Formspree may process and retain submissions under its own terms and privacy policy. The site itself does not save form entries to a local database, cookies, or browser storage. Your browser may retain entries through autofill or page restoration.

Connections made by your browser

The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google to request font files, which exposes connection information such as your IP address and browser request details to Google. See Google’s explanation of font privacy and its privacy policy.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The current site code does not set cookies, use analytics tools, or load advertising pixels. It does not sell or share form entries for advertising. See cookie settings for the current details.

External links

Instagram and other external sites have their own privacy practices. The Instagram link is an ordinary link, not an embedded feed; it does not load Instagram content into this site.

Your choices and questions

You can choose not to fill out the form, manage autofill and stored site data in your browser, and avoid opening external links. For privacy questions, contact: shapedbycode@gmail.comContact Shaped by Code · 346-491-2131.

Changes

This policy will be updated when hosting-related data processing, analytics, or other services change. Review the date above for the latest version.
