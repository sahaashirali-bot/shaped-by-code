My pick

C2 — violet field, bone S, ink script.

Flipping the ground to full violet instead of near-black is what actually improves this over the original — violet is your one truly ownable color, so leading with it (rather than treating it as a small accent inside a black tile) makes the avatar unmistakably yours from across a page of other icons.

It also holds contrast best at 20px: bone-on-violet and ink-on-violet both stay crisp, where black-background versions start to feel like they're competing with a phone's own dark-mode chrome.
